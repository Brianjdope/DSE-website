The Problem

Overcrowding Hurts Everyone

Overcrowded Facilities

Athletes and non-athletes alike struggle with packed gyms. Competition for machines deters students from working out entirely.

Wasted Time

Students arrive at the gym only to find every machine taken, with no way to know ahead of time how busy the facility is.

Health Impact

When students can't access facilities, it impacts both their physical fitness and emotional well-being during a critical time in their lives.

Key Insights

What Students Actually Want

01

Real-Time Visibility

Students want the ability to see how many people are using the pool, basketball courts, gym floor, and other facilities at any given time — before they leave their dorm.

02

Smarter Reservations

A reservation counter that displays how many students have booked a time slot, so users can pick less crowded windows and plan their workouts better.

03

Platform Accessibility

More accessibility across the entire DSE platform — making it easier for every student, athlete or not, to engage with campus recreation.

What We're Building

Core Feature

Reservation Counter

See how many students have booked each time slot before you reserve, so you can pick the least crowded window.

Push Notifications

Get reminders for upcoming reservations and instant alerts when cancellations open up a spot you wanted.

Schedule Integration

Up-to-date facility schedules maintained by DSE staff, so you always know what's open and when.

Cross-Platform

Works on iOS 15+ and Android 10+, reaching the entire Stevens student body on the devices they already carry.
